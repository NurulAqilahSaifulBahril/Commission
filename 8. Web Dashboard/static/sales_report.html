Sales Report Rules
Month
A case counts in the month of its first payment. From August 2026, the month that payment was created in the system.
NOC
One case per invoice. Duplicates, top-ups and cancelled invoices are left out.
ANS
Fully paid: the full invoice total, EPP included. Not fully paid yet: the Original Price.
RES / COM
COM: factory, kilang or commercial jobs, hotels, resorts, petrol stations and clinics. A shop lot of 36 panels or fewer is RES, more is COM. Anything tagged COMMERCIAL stays COM. Everything else is RES.
Shaded cell
Every customer in that month is fully paid.
Collected
Cash received on this year's invoices, up to the month picked above.
EP point
RES: 1 point per RM. COM before May 2026: 40%. COM from May 2026: the first RM40,000 at 100%, the rest at 40%.
May multiplier
Sales from 1 May to 10 June earn extra EP: +0.5 for every 3 cases in that window. Applies up to June only.
Confirmed figures
Months already confirmed with Finance stay fixed, even if the invoice is edited later.
