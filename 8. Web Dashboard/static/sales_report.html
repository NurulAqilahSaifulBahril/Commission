Sales Report Rules
Month
  • Month of 1st payment (paid date)
NOC
  • Number of cases or invoice
  • No duplicates, top-ups or cancelled
ANS
  • 100% paid: Commission Price (no EPP)
  • Below 100%: Sales Price (invoice total)
RES / COM
  • COM: factory, kilang, commercial, hotel, resort, petrol station, clinic
  • Shop lot: ≤ 36 panels RES, > 36 COM
  • Others: RES
Shaded cell
  • All customers 100% paid
Collected
  • Cash received up to the month picked
EP point
  • RES: 1 point per RM
  • COM before May: 40%
  • COM from May: first RM40k 100%, rest 40%
Late entry (from Aug)
  • Entered a later month than paid: no ANP and no Monthly Contest points
  • Still counts for EP, EGA/ESA and Early Bird
May multiplier
  • 1 May–10 Jun: +0.5 per 3 cases
  • Up to June only
EP multiplier
  • Oct: x1.5 EP on cases paid in Oct
  • Counts for ESA and Early Bird
  • Set on the Data page (EGA/ESA)
Confirmed months
  • Stay fixed
  • Basic & Net Floor Price Commission
  • ANP Commission
  • EGA/ESA Award
  • Production Bonus
  • Monthly Contest
