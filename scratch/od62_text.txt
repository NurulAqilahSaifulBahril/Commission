一、通告目的 
为配合公司业务持续发展及内部管理体系优化，恒浩能源有限公司现正式发布本阶段
销售激励及佣金（Commission）付款机制调整方案。 
本次调整旨在优化收入结构、提升资金效率，并进一步释放销售激励空间，让每一单

提升。 
我们相信：制度的优化，本质是为了让努力被更公平地放大与兑现 。

三、佣金管理政策（Commission Policy） 
为确保佣金制度公平性与可持续执行，公司将按以下原则管理： 
1. 佣金仅适用于已确认成交且实际收到款项之订单  
2. 佣金以实际到账金额及最终成交金额为计算基础  
3. 公司保留审核订单真实性及付款状态之权利  
4. 如涉及折扣、退款或价格调整，佣金将按最终成交金额重新核算  
5. 所有佣金统一按公司月度出佣流程执行  
 
四、佣金追回机制（Claw Back Policy） 
自 2026 年 7 月 1 日起，已发放佣金适用以下追回机制： 
1. 订单取消或终止 
公司有权追回已发放佣金（包括 RM300 预支款）。 
2. 客户付款违约 
如客户未按约定付款导致项目暂停或取消，公司有权暂停或追回相关佣金。 
3. 退款及价格调整 
如发生退款、折扣或价格调整，佣金将按最终成交金额重新计算，多发部分予以追
回。 
4. 离职或终止合作 
• 未发放佣金不予结算  
• 已发放预支佣金如不符合最终条件，公司保留追回权利

3. Commission Policy 
To ensure fairness and sustainability, the company will enforce the following principles: 
1. Commission is only applicable to confirmed deals with actual payments received  
2. Commission is calculated based on actual received amount and final transaction value  
3. The company reserves the right to verify transaction authenticity and payment status  
4. Any discounts, refunds, or price adjustments will result in commission recalculation 
based on final transaction value  
5. All commissions are processed under the company’s monthly payout cycle  
 
4. Clawback Policy 
Effective 1 July 2026, all paid commissions are subject to the following clawback rules: 
1. Order Cancellation or Termination 
The company reserves the right to recover any paid commissions, including the RM300 pre-
payment. 
2. Customer Payment Default 
If the customer fails to make payments as agreed, resulting in project suspension or 
cancellation, commissions may be suspended or recovered. 
3. Refunds or Price Adjustments 
If refunds, discounts, or pricing changes occur, commissions will be recalculated based on the 
final transaction value, and any excess will be recovered. 
4. Resignation or Termination 
• Unpaid commissions will not be processed
